## 3. Section copy mapped to the 8 video clusters

Each section opens with the pain/question the matching reels raise, shows the verified screen, and lands the section's CTA. The matching reel's CTA should land on this section (anchor).

### §A — Fragmentation (reels 01, 03, 10, 17)
**Heading:** Your health data is everywhere. And nowhere all at once.
**Body:** Sleep in one app. Medications in another. Reports in a drawer. HealthGraph is one place for the pieces of your health history — 20 health areas, from daily log to timeline, in a single app.
**Verified proof to show:** Explore grid (20 tiles) + Home dashboard ("Today Done 100%").
**Section CTA:** See how your health history could connect. → (stays on page, scrolls to §H)

### §B — AI / records (reels 02, 06, 11, 18)
**Heading:** Ask your records. Not a search bar.
**Body:** "How did I sleep this week?" Ask, instead of scrolling through PDFs. HealthGraph's chat works on your own history, and every answer can point to the record it came from. Wellness info only. Not medical advice.
**Verified proof to show:** AI chat screen (with the "Wellness info only. Not medical advice." line visible) + Records screen.
**Section CTA:** Ask your records. → in-app demo loop (video embed) or scroll to §D.
**Honest note (keep):** 2 AI messages per month are free; more is available with Premium (from €11/month, 3 days free — real, verified in-app).

### §C — Health Diary (reels 05, 08, 14)
**Heading:** Open a day from your health history.
**Body:** Every day, a paper-style report: what you logged, what you measured, what you recorded. Sorted by date, like a diary you can flip through. October 3: Pulse 59 BPM · Fair. Pulse — Fingertip 61 BPM · Fair. Log ×6.
**Verified proof to show:** Daily Report card ("3 OCT Today") + Daily Log screen.
**Section CTA:** Explore the health diary. → video embed of reel 14 (or 05/08).

### §D — Documents / structure (reels 04, 16)
**Heading:** From document to health history.
**Body:** Upload a report (PDF, PNG, JPG — up to 50 MB). It's not a pile of files: it becomes a structured event in your timeline. Your medical documents, part of your history.
**Verified proof to show:** Records upload screen ("Upload new / Past uploads") + Health Graph with the Visit node.
**Section CTA:** See how your health history could connect. → scroll to §H.

### §E — Measurements (reels 12, 13)
**Heading:** A measurement over time.
**Body:** One pulse reading means little. A series means something. Pulse check (fingertip PPG) saves results with quality and context: 67 BPM · FINGERTIP, 80.3 ms HRV RMSSD. Baseline builds after 4 morning readings. No diagnosis — your trend, in context.
**Verified proof to show:** Pulse measurement screen (full, values unaltered) + Health Graph time view.
**Section CTA:** Discover HealthGraph. → Google Play link.
**Boundary line (keep visible):** "HealthGraph shows your own measurements. It does not diagnose."

### §F — Medication (reel 07)
**Heading:** More than a reminder.
**Body:** One medication entry isn't your health history. Schedule it, take it, mark it — taken or not taken — and it stays in your record as part of what happened that day.
**Verified proof to show:** Home dashboard medication card + drawer (My Priorities / Medications).
**Section CTA:** Explore HealthGraph. → Google Play link.

### §G — Voice (reel 15)
**Heading:** A journal that answers back.
**Body:** Tap the red button, say how you are. Your check-in is stored as an entry in your history — and you can ask about it later. Wellness info only. Not medical advice.
**Verified proof to show:** Voice FAB on Home/Explore screens + AI chat.
**Section CTA:** Discover HealthGraph. → Google Play link.

### §H — Vision / brand (reels 09, 19, 20)
**Heading:** Your health isn't a spreadsheet.
**Body:** Rows and columns can't show you the connections. In HealthGraph, events, visits, heart, sleep and nutrition become nodes with links — 12 nodes · 22 connections — and you can drag through time. This is what your health history looks like when everything connects.
**Verified proof to show:** Health Graph screen + overview (Health Overview radar: REST 7h, MEALS 4, WATER 1.9L, HEART 59 bpm, WELLBEING 5/5).
**Section CTA:** Learn more at healthgraph.shop → (on site: scroll-to-top loop or Google Play). Final page footer CTA: Available on Google Play · Open beta.
